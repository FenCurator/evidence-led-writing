# Evidence-Led Writing Skill

Use this skill when writing reports, briefings, daily digests, long-form articles, social posts, or scripts that need to be engaging without overstating the evidence. It improves the writing process and claim calibration; it does not replace source research, fact-checking, or editorial judgment.

## Core Principle: Evidence Strength Sets Language Strength

**The certainty of a paragraph must not exceed the strength of its evidence.** This is the non-negotiable rule; every other technique operates inside this brake.

Label important claims internally:

- **[F] Fact** — directly stated by a source, with a URL or citation. Definitive wording is allowed.
- **[O] Observation** — a synthesis of multiple facts, with the time window and source set stated. Use wording such as “the pattern observed” or “several sources show”.
- **[I] Interpretation** — the writer’s judgment. Say “I infer” or “this may indicate”, and name alternative explanations.
- **[R] Recommendation** — only when the evidence could change a reader’s decision. Include conditions, cost, and verification status; otherwise present a candidate, not a conclusion.

**Write evidence before tone.** Do not invent a headline or conclusion and retrofit support afterward.

After drafting, scan upgrade words such as `first`, `proved`, `consensus`, `inevitable`, `represents`, `definitely`, `cut ties`, and `the obvious choice`. For each, ask: What is the source? What is an alternative explanation? Can the wording be cooled down?

## Hook

The first paragraph should create a reason to continue reading through suspense, emotion, or curiosity. Rotate among:

1. An action already underway.
2. A concrete time window containing several events.
3. A specific person, then widen to the theme.
4. An unusual or surprising detail.
5. A reversal or contradiction.
6. A meaningful, verifiable number.
7. A question the reader genuinely wants answered.

### Hook gates

- Before writing “today”, confirm that every included piece of evidence falls inside the stated time window.
- Every strong metaphor must be replaceable by a plain, verifiable sentence. If the plain sentence is unsupported, remove the metaphor.
- Quotation marks contain only verbatim text or a clearly marked translation. Paraphrases stay outside quotation marks.
- Do not open with a conclusion list, generic background, “today we will discuss”, or filler.

## People, Systems, and Specificity

Prefer a concrete person, company, event, system, dataset, standard, or failure pattern over an abstract label. Do not force a person into the lead when the real unit of change is a system or measurement.

Give the reader a judgment, not a transcript. Use concrete details and verifiable numbers instead of adjectives. Attribute numbers to their sources. Prefer strong verbs over vague verbs such as “involves” or “relates to”.

## Evidence Spine

Build each main line around five links:

> **Previous assessment → new evidence → counterevidence or alternative explanation → confidence change → decision relevance**

Use “because” only when a source directly supports causation. When multiple events merely co-occur, say that multiple sources point in the same direction rather than asserting causation.

Information without an arc belongs in a “weak signals” section or should be removed.

## Stakes and Action

Every piece should answer:

- Why does this matter now?
- What could the reader miss by ignoring it?
- How does it connect to the intended reader’s situation?

If no meaningful stake can be found, the piece may not deserve to be written.

AIDA is an optional distribution layer, not a fact-checking framework. Use it for summaries, social posts, and promotional copy when appropriate. Do not force a sales action: recommend an action only when evidence changes the reader’s choice, risk, or priority. Otherwise state that no action is currently needed.

## Layout for Scanning

- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use bold emphasis and bullets for scanability.
- Separate summaries or key-point boxes from the body.
- Embed links in the relevant line; put long URL lists at the end.
- Use the minimum complete length. Cut unsupported detail instead of padding a short piece.

## Honesty Gates

- Attach a source to every number and label the source type: official announcement, established media, secondary report, or signal-only.
- Absence of a signature or statement is evidence of absence only. Do not turn silence into opposition, a split, or marginalization; list plausible alternatives.
- Temporal order is not causation. Simultaneous events are not necessarily related.
- Do not invent motives or stories. Mark uncertain claims as unverified.

## Counter-Editor Pass

Before publication, answer:

1. Which simultaneous events did I accidentally turn into a causal claim?
2. Which sentence sounds most like clickbait? Does it still hold after removing the rhetoric?
3. Where could an opposing editor attack the numbers, source level, or definition most easily?

Fix the answer, not just the wording.

## Voice

Use short, clear sentences and an identifiable voice suited to the audience. The writer may be warm, sharp, playful, or restrained, but must make judgments and avoid bureaucratic filler. Metaphors can make abstractions concrete only after passing the evidence gate.

## Final Checklist

- [ ] The opening creates tension or curiosity rather than dumping the conclusion.
- [ ] Every “today” claim falls inside the stated time window.
- [ ] Every strong metaphor can be reduced to a verifiable plain sentence.
- [ ] Fact, observation, interpretation, and recommendation are distinct.
- [ ] No co-occurrence is presented as causation, one source as consensus, or silence as a position.
- [ ] Quoted text is verbatim or explicitly marked as translation.
- [ ] The main character is a concrete person, system, event, or measurement.
- [ ] Each main line has an evidence spine.
- [ ] The counter-editor’s strongest objection has been addressed.
- [ ] The ending includes an evidence-backed action or explicitly says no action is needed.
- [ ] Cutting roughly one third would remove redundancy, not evidence.
